How Claude Verifies Answers

To prevent hallucinations and fear-based claims, every answer goes through a dual-pass verification pipeline against our authentic shastra corpus.

🔍
Step 1

Corpus Retrieval

We search the local corpus (Mahabharata, Puranas, shlokas) and extract top matching passages with verifiable IDs.

✍️
Step 2

Claude Drafts

Call 1: Claude drafts an answer strictly from those passages, returning structured citations with exact quotes.

🛡️
Step 3

Claude Verifier

Call 2: A second Claude verifier audits the draft against the original passages to flag any unsupported statements.

✅
Step 4

Substring Check

Server-side checks verify quotes as exact substrings of retrieved text. Unsupported claims are removed before display.
