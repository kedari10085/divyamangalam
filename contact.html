Frequently Asked Questions

Are all services on Divya Mangalam free to use?

Yes. All scripture readers (the complete 12 volumes of the Mahabharata, 18 Mahapuranas), daily Vedic Panchang calendars, Shubh Muhurta calculators, and Dosha checkers are 100% free of charge. We are supported by non-intrusive Google AdSense advertising to cover hosting costs.

Is my birth or palm data stored on your servers?

No. All computational models (Panchang, Lagna, Dosha calculations, and palm reading dermatoglyphic tracing) run 100% client-side inside your own web browser. Your birth time, location, and photos are never uploaded or persisted on our servers.

Which edition of the Mahabharata is hosted on this website?

We host the complete, unabridged English prose translation by Pratap Chandra Roy, C.I.E. (published between 1883 and 1896 by the Oriental Publishing Co. in Calcutta), comprising all 18 Parvas across 12 volumes and 2,046 sections.
