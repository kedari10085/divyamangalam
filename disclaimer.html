Last updated: October 2026 · Important information regarding the educational and spiritual scope of our platform

1. General Informational & Cultural Purpose

The information, computational tools, astronomical almanacs, palm reading simulations, and scripture references provided on Divya Mangalam (divyamangalam.com) are published in good faith for cultural, educational, philosophical, and personal spiritual enrichment. All content is intended to foster appreciation for classical Sanatana Dharma traditions and ancient Indian sciences.

2. Astrological & Palmistry Readings

Vedic Astrology (Jyotish Shastra) and Hand Analysis (Hasta Samudrika Shastra) are ancient observational and interpretive systems rooted in classical treatises such as Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Phaladeepika, Saravali, and Garuda Purana. In accordance with digital publication ethics:

  • Calculations and interpretations (including Mangal Dosha, Kaal Sarp Dosha, Sade Sati, and Life/Head/Heart line readings) represent traditional astrological perspectives and should not be construed as absolute predictions of future occurrences.
  • Astrological interpretations reflect possibilities, tendencies, and symbolic cycles, but individual human agency, conscious effort (Purushartha), and free will remain the ultimate determinants of destiny.
  • No reading or analysis on this website should ever replace personal judgment or professional consultation.

3. Not Professional Medical, Legal, or Financial Advice

Divya Mangalam does not provide medical, psychological, psychiatric, legal, investment, or financial advisory services. Astrological remedies, gemstone suggestions, fasting recommendations, and planetary pacifications (Shantis) are traditional spiritual practices and must not be treated as substitutes for medical diagnoses, therapeutic interventions, or professional financial planning. Never ignore or postpone certified professional advice because of something you have read on this website.

4. Technical Calculation Accuracy & Planetary Ephemeris

Our Panchanga and astronomical calculators utilize modern browser-based celestial algorithms and the Lahiri Ayanamsa (Chitrapaksha) to compute sunrise, sunset, Tithis, Nakshatras, and planetary longitudes. While we strive for high mathematical fidelity matching classical ephemerides, local topographical differences, horizon variations, and micro-second discrepancies may occur. Users requiring exact muhurtas for sacred samskaras or temple consecrations are encouraged to corroborate with local temple panchangas.

5. Virtual Rituals & Devotional Symbology

Virtual Darshan, digital archana, and interactive visual pooja modules are spiritual aids designed to facilitate meditation, mental focus (Dharana), and devotional contemplation from home. They are symbolic digital expressions of reverence and are not represented as physical substitutes for personal temple visits, Vedic homams, or agamic rituals conducted by ordained priests.
