Om Suryaya Namah.

On this auspicious Ravivara, let the golden rays of Lord Surya Narayana illuminate your soul with boundless health, courage, and vitality.

As the eternal witness of the cosmos rises, offer your arghya with pure devotion and chant the sacred Aditya Hridaya Stotram to dispel all darkness from within.

Embrace today’s blessed Abhijit Muhurta at midday to begin noble endeavors, meditate, or seek divine grace, when the universe aligns in perfect harmony.

Remain mindful and avoid new ventures during Rahu Kaal in the late afternoon.

Surrender your fears to the divine light. May Bhagavan Surya bless your home with peace, strength, and radiant prosperity.

Shubham Bhavatu.
