Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Committed to safeguarding your privacy and personal data

1. Introduction

Welcome to Divya Mangalam (accessible at divyamangalam.com). Your privacy and peace of mind are of the utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy documents the types of personal information collected, recorded, and utilized by Divya Mangalam, as well as the choices available to you regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our digital platform, you may provide information in the following contexts:

  • Devotional Inputs: When using the Live Archana form or personalized prayer modules, you may optionally provide your name, Gotra, birth star (Nakshatra), Rashi, and prayer intention. This information is processed ephemerally within your active browser session to personalize your ritual display. We do not sell, rent, or trade devotee prayer data to third parties.
  • Astrological & Biometric Inputs: When using the Vedic Palm Reader, your hand photo is processed locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas client-side logic. Your palm image is never uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted to any server.
  • Contact Information: If you reach out via our contact form or direct email, we collect your name, email address, and message contents solely to address your inquiry.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We partner with Google AdSense to display advertisements across Divya Mangalam to support high-performance server hosting and sustain free access to our library of 2,046 Mahabharata chapters, 18 Mahapuranas, and interactive Vedic tools:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick / DART cookie) to serve ads on our site based on users' prior visits to divyamangalam.com and other websites across the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative at networkadvertising.org.

4. Cookies, Web Beacons & Log Files

Divya Mangalam follows standard industry procedures using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information. The purpose of this data is analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information to optimize reader experience.

5. Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

To provide a smooth, bookmarkable reading experience, Divya Mangalam uses your browser's private localStorage to remember:

  • Your last-read chapter index across the 12 Mahabharata volumes.
  • Your selected font size preferences for reading classical scriptures.
  • Audio volume preferences and temple bell toggle states.
  • Optional user-provided API keys for personal AI Pandit consultations (keys remain strictly inside your browser and are never transmitted to our servers).

6. GDPR Devotee Data Protection Rights (EU Users)

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), every user is entitled to the following rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing: You have the right to request that we restrict or cease processing your data.

7. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA Rights)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose what personal data it collects, request the deletion of consumer personal data, and demand that a business not sell their personal data. Divya Mangalam does not sell personal information under any circumstance.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Protecting children while using the internet is an essential priority. Divya Mangalam does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

9. Consent & Policy Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. We may update our Privacy Policy periodically; any amendments will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

For questions or requests concerning our privacy practices, please contact our data governance team via our Contact Page or write directly to contact@divyamangalam.com.
