Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Committed to safeguarding your devotional journey

1. Introduction

Welcome to Divya Mangalam (accessible at divyamangalam.com). Your privacy and spiritual peace of mind are of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

When you participate in our virtual services—such as Live Archana or the contact form—you may voluntarily provide your name, Gotra, birth date, time, and prayer intentions. This information is processed in your browser session to calculate your Nakshatra, Rashi, and personalize your devotional blessing. We do not sell or distribute personal devotee details to third parties.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website to support hosting and free devotional services for devotees worldwide:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

4. Local Browser Storage

Features such as the AI Pandit store your personal API keys or preference settings strictly inside your browser's private localStorage. These credentials never touch our servers or any public repositories.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page or write to contact@divyamangalam.com.
