Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hasta Samudrika Shastra & digital palm analysis

How accurate is digital Vedic palm reading? ▼
Our reading engine maps key landmarks based on classical principles of Hasta Samudrika Shastra (the ancient Vedic scripture of body markings). It identifies the Life Line (Ayur Rekha), Head Line (Matri Rekha), Heart Line (Pitri/Hridaya Rekha), and Fate Line (Karma Rekha). Accuracy depends on good lighting and a clear, flat palm.
Which hand should I scan? ▼
In Vedic tradition:
• Right Hand reflects Kriyaman Karma — the active destiny you are creating through choices, actions, and conscious effort.
• Left Hand reveals Prarabdha Karma — your innate potential, inherited spiritual tendencies, and subconscious blueprint.
Men typically scan their right hand for active readings, while women can scan either hand (traditionally left for innate traits, right for career).
Can the lines on my palm change over time? ▼
Yes! While the primary foundation remains stable, minor lines, branchings, and island markings shift constantly over months and years. In Vedic philosophy, your palm is a living mirror of your consciousness, spiritual practice (Sadhana), and righteous actions (Dharma).
Is my palm photo private and secure? ▼
Absolutely. Your photograph is processed 100% locally within your device's web browser using HTML5 Canvas. No image data is ever transmitted, uploaded, or saved to any external servers or third-party databases.
