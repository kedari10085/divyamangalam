Understanding Palmistry

What is Samudrika Shastra?

Samudrika Shastra is the ancient Indian text of body features study, originating from Vedic traditions. Hasta Samudrika specifically focuses on the hand, treating it as a mirror of the soul and a map of one's karmic journey.

Which hand should be read?

Traditionally, the dominant hand (usually the right) represents your active karma, conscious choices, and the life you are building. The non-dominant hand (usually the left) reveals your innate potential, subconscious traits, and past life influences.

Can my lines change?

Yes. While major lines rarely disappear, their depth, branches, and minor lines evolve constantly based on your thoughts, actions, and spiritual growth. The palm is a dynamic record of your consciousness.

How accurate is this digital reading?

This digital tool uses algorithms to map key areas of the hand based on general proportions and Vedic principles. While it offers profound traditional insights and self-reflection, it cannot replace the nuanced intuition of a living master.
