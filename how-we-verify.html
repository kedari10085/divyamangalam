How Claude Verifies Answers

We believe spiritual technology must never make unverified claims or generate fear-based predictions. Here is the exact technical pipeline we use to verify every AI Pandit answer against our classical Sanskrit corpus.

The 4-Step Verification Loop

From Source Corpus to Verified Citation

1

Corpus Retrieval

Local Shastra Search (Mahabharata, Puranas, Shlokas)

When you submit a query, our backend searches our indexed local corpus—including the 2,046 sections of the Mahabharata, the 18 Maha Puranas, and classical stotras. It identifies the top relevant passages, assigning each a verifiable passage ID (e.g. SHLOKA-GAYATRI, PUR-VISHNU-KEY-SHLOKA, MHB-VOL1-SEC1).

↓
2

Claude Drafts the Answer (Call 1)

Constrained Generation with Strict System Instructions

Claude is provided with only those retrieved passages and instructed to draft an answer strictly supported by the text. It must return structured JSON containing both the narrative and the exact passages cited. If the retrieved passages do not answer the question, Claude is explicitly commanded to state: "I could not find a source for this."

↓
3

Claude Verifier Pass (Call 2)

Independent Citation Audit

A second, separate Claude prompt acts as an auditor. It receives the draft answer, claimed citations, and original source passages. Its sole job is to categorize every claim into verified or unsupported. Any reasoning that leaps beyond the passages is flagged for removal.

↓
4

Server-Side Exact Substring Check

Deterministic Programmatic Validation

Before any response reaches your browser, our Node.js server executes an exact programmatic check: every quoted verse or phrase must appear as an exact substring of the passage retrieved from disk. If a citation fails this test, it is stripped. Only when this check passes does the user interface display the Verified against sources badge.

Honest Boundaries & What AI Cannot Do

While our verification pipeline significantly reduces false claims, language models can still make errors or misinterpret subtle contextual nuances.

  • Astrology is for reflection, not prediction: Vedic astrology (Jyotisha) is a traditional contemplative lens for self-understanding, duty (Dharma), and moral awareness. It cannot and should not predict your future deterministically.
  • No medical, legal, or financial advice: Divya Mangalam will never provide medical diagnoses, legal guidance, or investment advice. Always consult certified professionals for life decisions.
  • Human free will (Purushartha) is paramount: Classical Sanatana Dharma explicitly teaches that conscious right effort and ethical action supersede any astrological inclination.
