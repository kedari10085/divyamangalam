The Vision & Leadership

Founder Profile: Kedarisai Abhishek

🧘 Architect

Kedarisai Abhishek

Founder & Lead Engineer

Kedarisai Abhishek is a technologist and spiritual researcher dedicated to uniting classical Indian knowledge systems with state-of-the-art AI. Frustrated by commercial astrology portals that profit from anxiety and superstition, he envisioned a modern platform combining mathematical astronomical ephemeris with the deep reasoning and ethical alignment of Anthropic Claude.

Under his architecture, Divya Mangalam has digitized over 2,046 sections of the Mahabharata, indexed the 18 Maha Puranas, implemented client-side astronomical calculators for Panchang and Muhurat, and developed real-time computer vision for Hasta Samudrika palmistry—all while adhering to strict privacy and zero data-brokering standards.

Our Guiding Philosophy

Four Non-Negotiable Pillars

📜

Canonical Shastric Rigor

We never fabricate remedies. Everything is anchored in classical treatises: Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Surya Siddhanta, and Vedanga Jyotisha.

⚡

Purushartha Over Fatalism

Vedic thought declares that free will (Kriyamana Karma) is supreme. We use Claude to empower conscious human action, never to instill paralysis or dread.

🔒

100% Client-Side Privacy

Birth coordinates and biometric palm scans are calculated directly inside your browser memory. We reject third-party data tracking of sacred life moments.

📖

Permanent Open Access

The sacred 2,046 sections of the Mahabharata, the 18 Maha Puranas, and daily Panchang calculations remain free forever for seekers and scholars globally.
