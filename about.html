Who We Are

A Temple of Light and Grace

Divya Mangalam is a devotional temple dedicated to serving the spiritual needs of all devotees — whether they visit in person or connect virtually. The temple is consecrated to Maa Lakshmi, the goddess of prosperity, love, and auspiciousness.

For generations, this temple has been a beacon of light for the community. Every puja performed here carries with it the accumulated devotion of thousands of worshippers, creating an atmosphere of pure, transformative grace.

Through our Virtual Darshan and Online Archana services, we extend the temple's blessings across the world, making divine connection possible from wherever you are.

Presiding Deity

Maa Lakshmi – Goddess of Auspiciousness

Maa Lakshmi bestows wealth, health, beauty, and liberation. She is worshipped as Adishakti, the primordial energy that sustains the universe.
