WHO WE ARE

Preserving Sanatana Dharma for the Digital Era

Divya Mangalam was established as an open-access Vedic knowledge initiative with a singular mission: to make the profound wisdom of Sanatana Dharma accessible, readable, and mathematically verifiable for seekers, scholars, and devotees across the globe.

Too often, sacred Indian texts exist either behind paywalls, in fragmented scans, or buried in inaccessible archives. We have painstakingly curated, digitized, and structured complete primary sources—including the unabridged 2,046 sections of Pratap Chandra Roy's English prose translation of the Srimad Mahabharata across all 18 Parvas, the 18 Maha Puranas, and the astronomical treatise of Sage Lagadha's Vedanga Jyotisha.

Alongside sacred literature, Divya Mangalam develops high-fidelity, client-side astronomical calculators for Vedic Panchang, Shubh Muhurta, and classical Dosha analysis (Mangal, Kaal Sarp, Sade Sati, Pitra) running entirely in the browser with zero server data tracking.

AUTHENTICITY & SCHOLARSHIP

Anchored in Revered Classical Treatises

Our computational algorithms, astrological frameworks, and textual libraries are directly derived from authoritative classical Sanskrit literature:

Vedanga Jyotisha

SAGE LAGADHA (~1200 BCE)

The foundational astronomical limb of the Vedas establishing the 5-year Yuga, Ayana (solstitial) turning points, and solar-lunar synchrony.

Brihat Parashara Hora

MAHARISHI PARASHARA

The authoritative manual of Vedic natal astrology, detailing planetary dignities, Bhavas, Kuja (Mangal) Dosha rules, and classical Shanti remedies.

Muhurta Chintamani

DAIVAJNA RAMACHANDRA

The premier handbook on electional astrology, providing specific guidelines for marriage, housewarming, business inaugurations, and Dasa Koota.

Hasta Samudrika Shastra

GARUDA & SKANDA PURANAS

The ancient Vedic dermatoglyphic science interpreting hand contours, primary palm lines, and the seven planetary mounts as karmic maps.
