Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Divya Mangalam subscriptions and data ethics.

Will the scriptures and Panchang remain free?

Yes, permanently. Divya Mangalam was founded on an open-access commitment to preserve Sanatana Dharma. The 2,046 sections of the Mahabharata and Panchang will always be free.

How does Claude ensure accuracy in astrology?

Claude does not guess your planetary positions. Our astronomy engine calculates exact degrees via Lahiri Ayanamsa, and Claude synthesizes canonical rules from Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra.

Is my birth data or palm photo sold or shared?

Never. Your natal coordinates and palm images are processed securely and never sold to third-party ad networks or brokers. Palm line extraction runs directly in your browser.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription at any time?

Yes, with a single click. There are no lock-in contracts or hidden cancellation fees. If you cancel, your Pro features remain active until the end of your billing cycle.
