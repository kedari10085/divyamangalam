Kalaprakasika Chapters XIV & XXXIII

ऊर्ध्व, अधो, तिर्यङ्मुख नक्षत्राणि — Stellar Motion Facing

Every star gazes upward, downward, or horizontally. Aligning the physical orientation of human actions with the stellar gaze unlocks effortless cosmic resonance and success.

⬆️

ऊर्ध्वमुख (Oordhwa-Mukha)

Upward-Facing Stars (9 Stars)

Auspicious for ascending, erecting, growing, and elevating works:

⭐ Rohini ⭐ Uttara Phalguni ⭐ Uttara Ashadha ⭐ Uttara Bhadrapada ⭐ Pushya ⭐ Shravana ⭐ Dhanishtha ⭐ Shatabhisha ⭐ Ardra
Best Undertakings:
  • Griha Pravesh & house foundation
  • Temple construction & hoisting sacred flags
  • Coronation, appointments & inaugurations
  • Planting tall fruit-bearing trees & gardens
⬇️

अधोमुख (Atho-Mukha)

Downward-Facing Stars (9 Stars)

Auspicious for subterranean, excavating, and deep earth works:

⭐ Bharani ⭐ Krittika ⭐ Ashlesha ⭐ Magha ⭐ Purva Phalguni ⭐ Vishakha ⭐ Moola ⭐ Purva Ashadha ⭐ Purva Bhadrapada
Best Undertakings:
  • Digging water wells, borewells, tanks & ponds
  • Underground vaults, cellars & treasure chambers
  • Mining, boring, and geological excavation
  • Setting permanent property boundary pillars
➡️

तिर्यङ्मुख (Thiryag-Mukha)

Horizontal-Facing Stars (9 Stars)

Auspicious for horizontal transit, voyages, vehicles, and pathways:

⭐ Ashwini ⭐ Mrigashira ⭐ Punarvasu ⭐ Hasta ⭐ Chitra ⭐ Swati ⭐ Anuradha ⭐ Jyeshtha ⭐ Revati
Best Undertakings:
  • Purchasing and taking delivery of cars & vehicles
  • Embarking on journeys, voyages & pilgrimages (Yatra)
  • Laying roads, canals, bridges and pipelines
  • Starting trade caravans, navigation & dispatching shipments

🛡️ Neutralization of Adverse Yogas (Kalaprakasika Chapter XXXIV: दोष परिहारम्)

यथा तमः प्रविशति दृष्ट्वा सूर्यसमुद्भवम् । तथा नश्यन्ति दोषाणि गुरुणा केन्द्रे स्थितेन वा ॥

"Just as the darkness of the entire world is vanquished the moment the glorious Sun rises upon the horizon, even so do all millions of adverse doshas perish when Jupiter (Guru / Brihaspati) or Venus (Shukra) is strongly situated in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) or Trikona (5th, 9th) house in the election lagna."

Sage Narasimha further proclaims that an unafflicted Moon in the 11th house (Labha Sthana) or an exalted benefic planet aspecting the Lagna neutralizes adverse Tithi blemishes, Vishaghatika, and planetary combustions.

🪙 Classical Rules for Purchasing Gold & Land (Daivajna Rama)

Gold & Precious Jewelry (Swarna Kharid): As per Muhurta Chintamani, gold should be purchased under Laghu / Kshipra stars (Pushya, Hasta, Ashwini) or Mridu stars (Chitra, Revati, Anuradha). Special yogas like Ravi Pushya Yoga (Sunday + Pushya) and Guru Pushya Yoga (Thursday + Pushya) bestow inexhaustible Mahalakshmi blessings. Saturday is strictly avoided for buying gold.

Land & Real Estate (Bhoomi / Sampatti Kharid): Permanent property agreements and registry must be undertaken in Dhruva / Sthira stars (Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada) so that the land remains permanently in one's possession and appreciates multifold. Aligning with Tripushkar Yoga multiplies investment returns three-fold!
