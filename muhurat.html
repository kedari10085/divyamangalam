Authored by Daivajña Rāma (दैवज्ञ राम, c. 1600 CE), the Nakshatra Prakarana classifies all 27 stars into 7 sacred Ganas, establishing which human endeavors bear fruit under specific planetary constellations.

🪙 Classical Rules for Purchasing Gold & Land (Daivajna Rama)

Gold & Precious Jewelry (Swarna Kharid): As per Muhurta Chintamani, gold should be purchased under Laghu / Kshipra stars (Pushya, Hasta, Ashwini) or Mridu stars (Chitra, Revati, Anuradha). Special yogas like Ravi Pushya Yoga (Sunday + Pushya) and Guru Pushya Yoga (Thursday + Pushya) bestow inexhaustible Mahalakshmi blessings. Saturday is strictly avoided for buying gold.

Land & Real Estate (Bhoomi / Sampatti Kharid): Permanent property agreements and registry must be undertaken in Dhruva / Sthira stars (Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada) so that the land remains permanently in one's possession and appreciates multifold. Aligning with Tripushkar Yoga multiplies investment returns three-fold!
